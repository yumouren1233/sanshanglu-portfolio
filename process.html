Methodology

Product Development
Workflow

A systematic approach to transforming ideas into manufacturable products. Each phase builds on the previous, ensuring design decisions are grounded in feasibility and user needs.

Market & Competitor Analysis

Understand the landscape. Identify gaps, benchmark existing products, and define where the opportunity lies before any design work begins.

User Positioning

Define target users, their pain points, usage scenarios, and willingness to pay. Build personas that drive design decisions throughout the project.

Concept Sketching

Rapid visual exploration of multiple form directions. Evaluate concepts against user needs, manufacturing constraints, and brand positioning.

3D Modeling (Rhino)

Translate selected concepts into precise 3D models. Focus on surface quality, part relationships, and design intent preservation from sketch to digital.

Manufacturability Evaluation

Assess draft angles, wall thickness, undercuts, and assembly logic. Identify production risks early and iterate the design for feasible manufacturing.

Prototyping & Testing

Physical prototypes validate form, fit, and function. Test with real users and refine based on feedback before committing to tooling.

Iteration & Production Optimization

Final refinements based on prototype learnings. Optimize for cost, quality, and scalability. Prepare production-ready documentation for factory handoff.
